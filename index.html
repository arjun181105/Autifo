02Questions

The "what does this mean" section.

Clear answers before we talk about the right system for your business.

What does Autifo actually build? +

Custom digital solutions for property and mortgage businesses. That can be a conversion-focused or personal brand website, Meta ads and marketing, a speed-to-lead system, a custom AI system or geospatial chatbot, CRM and workflow integrations, or the right combination.

Why specialise in property and mortgage? +

Because agents, agencies, brokers and brokerages share high-value lead journeys where speed, trust, follow-up and clean handoffs matter. Specialisation lets us design around those realities instead of selling generic automation.

Is this just a generic AI chatbot service? +

No. We first understand the business, workflow and bottleneck. If an AI agent is the right solution, it gets a clear job, the right integrations, sensible guardrails and a defined handoff to the team.

Can we start with one part of the system? +

Yes. Speed-to-lead, a website or one focused agent can work independently. We recommend the highest-ROI starting point and make sure it can connect cleanly to later stages.

Will Autifo work with our existing CRM and tools? +

Where practical, yes. We assess what already works and connect the solution to your website, forms, inbox, calendar, CRM, phone or messaging tools rather than forcing unnecessary replacement.

How much does an Autifo solution cost? +

Pricing is custom — based on the scope, integrations and complexity. The strategy call identifies the business case first, then we provide a clear recommendation and scope.

Who is Autifo best suited to? +

Real estate agents, agencies, mortgage brokers and brokerages with meaningful lead flow, follow-up friction or repetitive processes — and a clear reason to improve how opportunities move through the business.
