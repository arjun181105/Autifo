07Questions

Clear answers before we talk.

If the answer depends on your workflow, that is exactly what the strategy call is designed to uncover.

What does Autifo actually sell?+

A custom digital solution designed around the most valuable bottleneck in your property or mortgage business. That may be a conversion-focused website, a speed-to-lead system, a custom AI agent, Meta Ads infrastructure or a connected combination.

Do we have to replace our current CRM or tools?+

Not necessarily. We first assess what already works. Where practical, the solution connects to your current website, forms, inbox, calendar, CRM and messaging tools rather than forcing a complete rebuild.

Is this just an AI chatbot?+

No. Autifo is not positioned around generic chatbots. An AI agent is only useful when it has a clear job, the right workflow, appropriate guardrails and a defined handoff to the team.

Can we start with one part of the system?+

Yes. Speed-to-lead, a website or a focused automation can work independently. We prioritise the highest-ROI starting point and design it so future stages can connect cleanly.

How is pricing decided?+

Pricing depends on the scope, integrations and complexity of the solution. The strategy call identifies the business case first, then we provide a clear recommendation and scope before work begins.

Who is Autifo best suited to?+

Real estate agents, agencies, mortgage brokers and brokerages with meaningful lead flow, follow-up friction or repetitive processes—and a clear reason to improve how those opportunities move through the business.
